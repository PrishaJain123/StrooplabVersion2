StroopLab V2

Assistance & perceived agency

About this experiment

This version builds on the original Stroop task and explores how different levels of computer assistance relate to performance and perceived control.

Practice10 trials
Main task72 trials
Conditions3 × 24 trials

Your task: identify the ink colour, not the word. In the main task, you will complete three short blocks:

  • Unaided: choose the ink-colour key yourself.
  • Suggestion: the computer displays a suggested ink colour; you remain free to choose.
  • Delegated: the computer selects the response and you confirm it with Space. This block measures confirmation time, not ordinary choice reaction time.

After each block, you will rate your perceived control, sense of ownership, and trust in the assistance.

For supervised research use: this is a student project prototype, not an ethics-approved study. Before recruiting anyone else, obtain teacher/school approval and the appropriate informed consent and parent/guardian permission for participants under 18. Participation must be voluntary. Do not enter names, email addresses, or other identifying details. You can use the demo yourself without collecting anyone else's data.

Privacy: all trial data stays in this browser tab until you choose to download the CSV. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
