# StroopLab V2 — Limitations and reproducibility

- This is a prototype, not a validated or ethics-approved research instrument.
- The three blocks are completed in randomized order, but practice, fatigue, and carry-over effects may remain.
- Suggestions are always correct. This does not test how people respond to inaccurate or unreliable automation.
- Delegated trials require Space confirmation, so their latency is a different measure from the R/G/B/Y choice reaction time in the other conditions.
- Delegated accuracy reflects the computer's selection, not participant decision accuracy.
- The three block ratings are brief, unvalidated single-item self-reports. They should not be presented as validated measures of agency or trust.
- The application is designed for a desktop keyboard. Browser, keyboard, display, and device differences can affect timing.
- Data is held in page memory and is not uploaded. A reload or closed tab can discard an unexported session.
- No identifying information is collected. Keep downloaded data secure and follow school requirements for consent, storage, retention, and deletion.
- Obtain appropriate school/teacher review and informed consent, including parent/guardian permission for under-18 participants where required, before any participant recruitment.
